Print Quote Calculator

Work out what a print costs you and what to charge a friend. The starting numbers are an example sized for a Bambu Lab P1S: change anything to match your own print, filament and electricity rate. Your changes are kept in this browser.

The print

Copy the time and filament weight from your slicer's estimate for one copy.

Filament used, per copy

Use one row per material or colour.

Running costs

Average power is an estimate, so a plug-in energy meter on the printer gives a truer number. Machine wear spreads the printer price over its lifetime hours. Failures and waste covers failed prints, purge and supports, as a share of filament, power and wear.

Your time and profit

Hands-on time is slicing, loading, removing the print and cleaning it up. A 30% margin means 30% of the price you charge is profit. In whole plate mode, finishing is the per-part time for removing supports and tidying each part.

If a plate fails

Looks at the whole order. It assumes one plate fails and any other plates come out fine. It counts the full cost of the good plates, filament, power and wear on the failed plate up to the point it stopped, your setup time and finishing for the good parts, then shows what you are left with and what reprinting the missing parts would do.

Quote details for the PDF

The PDF shows the item, quantity and price only. Your costs and profit are never included.
